Education System & Cost Sharing

Ethiopian Education System: 8-4-4 system (8 years primary, 4 years secondary, 4 years tertiary). Our journey at Wolaita Sodo University is part of the national commitment to produce competent engineers.

Cost Sharing: The Ethiopian government introduced cost-sharing to expand access to higher education. We are grateful for the opportunity and financial support that made our engineering education possible.

We thank the government of Ethiopia for investing in our future and for the chance to serve our nation.
